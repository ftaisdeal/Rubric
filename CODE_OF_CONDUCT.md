# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Trolling, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at firinn.taisdeal@gmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
